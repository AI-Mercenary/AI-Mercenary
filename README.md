## Featured Projects

### 🧠 [Enterprise Document Search Assistant](https://github.com/AI-Mercenary/My_Projects)
Internal knowledge assistant built with a RAG pipeline — dense-vector indexing, semantic chunking, and prompt calibration on top of Gemini 2.0 Flash. Reduced document-search time by **70%**, achieved **70% top-1 retrieval precision**, and cut off-topic results by **60%** across 120+ files.

`Python` `Gemini 2.0 Flash` `MongoDB` `Streamlit` `RAG` `Prompt Engineering` `NLP`

---

### 🤖 AI Fitness Coach
Agentic fitness platform with a sequential LLM reasoning chain in LangGraph — structured goal-parsing feeds into plan-synthesis for personalized workout and nutrition output. Achieved **3× faster generation** vs. naive prompting. Full-stack delivery with a React.js frontend and Node.js/Express.js backend.

`Python` `LangChain` `LangGraph` `React.js` `Node.js` `Express.js` `OpenAI API`

---

### 🎬 [Video Summarizer](https://github.com/AI-Mercenary/Video_Summarizer_Project)
Temporal attention–based video summarization pipeline that condenses long-form video to under 10% of original length. Integrated audio and text metadata for higher accuracy. Built end-to-end during a **72-hour AI/ML hackathon**.

`Python` `PyTorch` `Transformers` `NLP` `OpenCV` `yt-dlp`
